## Q1 is the one that did not resolve, and why it matters

The question was whether Textron paid **$300,000** or **$11 million** for
Spectrolab in 1960 — a 37× conflict that decides whether Mann is in the study.

Perplexity returned the exact $11M sentence with its URL. But that sentence is
the International Directory of Company Histories claim **this row already
carried**. It could not reach the contemporaneous *Los Angeles Times* of 2
August 1960 (behind a Newspapers.com archive), which is the source West's paper
cites for $300,000, and it found no Spectrolab revenue figure for any year
1956–1960.

So the conflict stands, and the more contemporaneous source is still the one
saying $300,000. **Mann stays excluded.** Taking the figure that happens to make
a row includable, over a closer source that contradicts it, is precisely the
failure this study spent the most effort avoiding.

For the record: $11M would clear the 1960 `acq50` bar of $4,597,087 even split
across the two companies; $300,000 would not. Re-open only if someone reads the
LA Times page itself.

## What Q2 and Q3 changed, short of a rescue

Neither closed its bracket, but both improved the *quality* of an exclusion:

- **Biocon** now has an above-bar figure at 1997 rather than 1998. The lower end
  is still the 1978 founding, so the span is 19 years against a 10-year cap. A
  figure *below* bar in any year 1978–1996 would rescue it. Note also that
  "total income" overstates revenue, so even 1997 is an upper bound.
- **Hansoh** now has a genuine sourced below-bar anchor: RMB 30M in 1997,
  ~US$3.62M against a $4,985,355 bar, and company-level rather than
  product-only, which is exactly what the question asked for. Against the
  earliest above-bar figure at 2011 that is a 14-year span. It remains excluded
  but is now a *bounded* exclusion instead of an open one. Any company figure
  between 1998 and 2010 closes it.

## The correction that was applied

Q6 surfaced a chronology conflict. Century Bond's 2004 annual report dates Ready
Chemical to 1985 and Multiview to 1986; Farm Fresh Berhad's FY2024 director
profile — the source behind p147's `a4` — says both in 1984. A contemporaneous
report is normally the stronger source, so **`a4` confidence was lowered from
high to medium and the conflict left visible** rather than silently resolved,
which is how this study has handled source conflicts elsewhere. `a4` is not an
input to the headline clock, so nothing moved.
