# Superseded interim reports

**These are not the study's results.** The results are `analysis/paper.tex` and
`analysis/paper-ko.tex`.

These two reports were written on 22 August 2026, when the study had researched
**160 people** and had a revenue-strict n of 60. Collection ended the next day
at 235 people and n=93. Any number quoted from these files is stale.

They are kept, rather than deleted, because they are evidence.

## Why they are worth keeping

The final estimate stopped on a boundary: the CI half-width landed exactly on
the rule's 1.0-year limit. The obvious criticism of any boundary stop is
optional stopping — that collection continued until the number looked good and
then halted.

These reports are a dated, committed snapshot from before the last two waves,
and they already report a median of **9.2 years**. The final figure is **9.0**.
The estimate moved by 0.2 years while the revenue-strict sample grew from 60 to
93, and the full wave-by-wave history — 10.0, 9.0, 8.8, 9.0, 9.0, 9.8, 9.2,
9.0, 9.0 — shows the same stability from roughly the fortieth person onward.

A study cannot prove it did not stop opportunistically by asserting it. It can
show a timestamped earlier version of the same number. That is what these are
for, and it is why deleting them would have cost more than the clutter they
save.
