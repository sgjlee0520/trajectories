## Method notes (applies across entries)

1. **Bars.** Table years used as printed. Off-table years: linear interpolation between neighbours. Example used above: 1989 = 3,342,206 + 0.8 × (4,059,724 − 3,342,206) = **$3,916,220**; 2009 = 6,066,291 + 0.8 × (6,773,124 − 6,066,291) = **$6,631,757**; 1981 = 2,559,459 + 0.2 × (3,342,206 − 2,559,459) = **$2,716,008**; 2004 = 5,348,772 + 0.8 × (6,066,291 − 5,348,772) = **$5,922,788**.
2. **FX.** World Bank official period-average LCU per USD (`PA.NUS.FCRF`), not later-year conversions embedded in retrospective interviews (the HCL Wharton “$22 million” for 1989 is the cautionary example).
3. **IPO / acquisition years** were not used as crossings.
4. **unknown** is used where (a) the first sourced above-bar year is more than 10 years after the last sourced below-bar/founding bound (HCL), or (b) the company still has not reached the bar in the latest sourced accounts (Passion Capital).
